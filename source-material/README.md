# Source material

Working papers written for this effort, brought here one file at a time because they are safe to publish. They are the documents the site's figures and claims rest on, kept so anyone can check our work. They were written for Brian and for the leadership of his church while the work was being decided, so they read as working notes, not as finished pages: recommendations, open questions, and figures that later papers revised. Later papers supersede earlier ones where they disagree; the site itself carries the current figures.

Each paper keeps its original date and number. The numbering has gaps because not every paper is publishable (see "What is not here").

## How the papers were prepared for publication

Every paper was copied from Brian's working folder and then edited in these ways, and only these:

- Private individuals are referred to by role or relationship, never by name: "our representative," "friends in Ibadan," "a friend of Brian's." People named in published press interviews and studies keep the names the press printed.
- The church is referred to as "the church" or "Brian's church" rather than by name.
- Any figure for what a helper or representative might be paid has been removed.
- Working-file paths have been removed. Research files cited by code (NG1, V1, L1, P7, and so on) are Brian's private research notes, each with numbered sources and dates accessed; they are available on request.
- Paper 25 originally checked figures from two Lagos schools for the blind. The sections about the school we did not go forward with are left out, and the paper says so at the top.

Nothing else was changed: no figure, source, date, conclusion, or wording beyond the substitutions above.
