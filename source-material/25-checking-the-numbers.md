# Checking the Numbers from Bethesda

Prepared for Brian, September 25, 2026. An earlier version of this note also checked figures from a second Lagos school for the blind that we corresponded with at the time; those sections are left out here because we did not go forward with that school, and nothing about Bethesda's figures depended on them. Three research passes and one independent verification pass (files V1 through V4 in the research folder), all sources dated September 25. This note replaces the "trust their numbers for now" stance in the September 24 comparison with a verdict on each figure, a dollar value, and a planning figure we can use in front of other people.

## How to read the dollar amounts

Two exchange rates are in play. The Central Bank of Nigeria rate on the days checked was about ₦1,330 to the dollar; the Lagos street rate was about ₦1,390. Bethesda's ₦1,400 is slightly above both. A church wire or a service such as Wise or Remitly lands near the bank rate, so ₦1,330 is the rate the church's money will actually get, and every dollar figure below uses it; the street rate would make each figure about four percent smaller. On the two figures that matter most this changes almost nothing, but it means Bethesda's own dollar equivalents run a little low.

## The verdicts, one figure at a time

Bethesda: University of Lagos tuition ₦252,000 a year ($190). Plausible, not confirmed. UNILAG's own fee notice, in force since the 2023/2024 session and re-checked this week, sets obligatory charges for a returning student at ₦100,750 to ₦190,250 plus ₦20,000 utilities, and for a new student at ₦126,325 to ₦176,300 plus ₦30,000 in one-time charges. No official total reaches ₦252,000, but faculty and departmental levies, dues, and identity and library charges sit outside that notice, and a real bill in that range is believable. No later official revision was found. Bethesda's "it was ₦15,000 when we started" fits the well-documented history of federal university fees before 2023. Planning figure: ₦252,000, described as "about $190 including faculty charges," until Bethesda shows one student's fee receipt.

Housing for a UNILAG student. UNILAG's official bed space was ₦43,000 in 2024 and was raised to ₦80,000 ($60) in the latest reporting, with a few specialty halls at ₦250,000 to ₦500,000. Private hostels around the campus run ₦250,000 to ₦700,000. Most students cannot get one of the university's scarce bed spaces and rent privately. Planning figure: ₦500,000 unless an official bed space can be secured for the student, in which case ₦80,000. This is the single largest swing in a sponsorship budget and is worth one direct question: does a blind student get priority for a university hostel?

Bethesda: ₦50,000 a month per sponsored student for photocopying and printing ($38 a month, $450 a year). Inflated as a printing budget, reasonable as a living allowance. A 2026 Nigerian student budgeting guide puts a sighted student's printing and academic materials at ₦2,000 to ₦8,000 a month and a full lean month of living in Lagos, feeding included, at about ₦50,000. Bethesda's figure is six to twenty-five times the printing line and roughly equal to a whole month's living costs. A blind student does pay more for materials (scanning, embossing, paying readers), and no Nigerian price for those was found, but ₦50,000 a month is best understood as a general stipend that Bethesda labels modestly. That is not a mark against them; it is a reason to describe it accurately to donors as "a monthly allowance."

Bethesda: 175 sponsored students nationwide, and the ₦1,400 rate. The 175 is their own count and cannot be checked from outside; it is larger than the 126 residents counted in our earlier notes, which described the home, not the sponsorship program. The ₦1,400 is about one percent above the street rate and five percent above the bank rate; harmless, but we should convert at ₦1,330.

## Our own figures, re-checked

Used laptops in Lagos. Confirmed. Live listings this week for the class of machine we specified (Dell Latitude and Lenovo ThinkPad, Core i5, 8 gigabytes, 256-gigabyte solid-state drive) run ₦180,000 to ₦270,000 from Computer Village-type sellers, typical ₦220,000 to ₦250,000 ($165 to $190). The same machines through Jumia's marketplace cost about double, ₦420,000 to ₦490,000, so whoever buys should buy in person or through a trusted dealer, not the marketplace. Windows 11-capable machines (8th generation and up) cost ₦40,000 to ₦80,000 more, and NVDA does not need them, so the older machines are the right buy. A new entry-level laptop is ₦195,000 to ₦230,000 but with a weaker processor and half the storage, so used business machines remain the better value. Planning figure: ₦240,000 ($180) per laptop bought in Lagos, which is close to Brian's $150 refurbished figure here before shipping and duties.

## Planning figures to use from here

A year of university sponsorship for one blind student in Lagos, Bethesda-style and all in: tuition and charges ₦252,000, private hostel ₦500,000, monthly allowance ₦600,000, about ₦1,350,000, which is $1,015 at the bank rate. A lean version with a university bed space and no allowance is about ₦332,000, $250. Our earlier $350 to $450 figure is withdrawn. One-time devices (laptop, phone, cane, recorder) are on top and would be perhaps ₦400,000 to ₦500,000 if we supplied a laptop and cane and left the phone to the family.

Laptops: ₦240,000 ($180) each bought in Lagos.

Exchange rate: ₦1,330. Round dollar figures up, never down, when speaking to the congregation.

## Are they inflating?

No. Every number either checks out, sits inside a documented range, or is ambiguous in a way a single question resolves. The figure that looked high (the ₦50,000 monthly allowance) turns out to describe a real cost under an honest but imprecise label: a living stipend called a printing allowance. Where our own earlier figures were wrong, they were wrong on the low side, which is the usual failure of pricing from list prices instead of receipts.

## Questions that still have to go back

To Bethesda: what does one sponsored university student cost you for a full year, all in, and could you show one student's fee receipt; is the ₦50,000 monthly allowance intended for printing only or for living costs; and how do you receive gifts from abroad.
